🎭 How to Host

Horrible Experts: Interviewer knows the absurd profession. Interviewee discovers it through the interview and must act like an expert.

Change!: Two performers share a scene. When the host says CHANGE!, the performer replaces their last line or action.

Beat the Scene: Choose a musical scenario; rap, sing, or speak rhythmically while advancing the story. Choose a bundled instrumental in the Beat Deck (or use your own).

Class Dismissed!: One teacher faces performers with secret characters. Reveal each character privately, then let the teacher discover who they are through the scene.

BUZZ! is neutral punctuation: hilarious peak, self-buzz, mercy ending, or stumped performer. TWIST! adds a complication. Points are entirely the host's call. Use the ACT'N OUT! award on either team to grant any amount of points for any reason. The timer is optional.

For private prompts, the public screen must not show the secret. Hide the screen from the guesser during assignment, or use paper slips. There is no remote host screen in this standalone version.
